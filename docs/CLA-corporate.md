Project must have on file a signed Contributor License Agreement ("CLA") from

This version of the Agreement allows an entity (the "Corporation") to submit
Contributions to the Licensor, to authorize Contributions submitted by its
designated employees to the Licensor, and to grant copyright and patent

course of employment; an individual contributing on their own behalf signs
`docs/CLA-individual.md` instead.

`LICENSE.md` and `NOTICE`. Throughout this Agreement, "the Licensor" means that

Contributor License Agreement, adapted for this Project. The Apache
definitions, copyright grant, patent grant, representations and disclaimer are
preserved; the preamble, the proprietary-relicensing paragraph in section 2,
the authorization clause, the applicable-law clause, the schedules and the
